 1965: When Dylan plugged in

 On this day in 1965,[Bob Dylan](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Bob-Dylan)walked onstage at the[Newport Folk Festival](https://www.britannica.com/art/Newport-Folk-Festival)and changed popular music. His acoustic songs, filled with poetic lyrics and social commentary, had made him a hero to [folk](https://www.britannica.com/art/folk-music) purists. But he was moving in a different direction, blending traditional folk songwriting with electric instruments. While the reaction at Newport was mixed—some booed, while others cheered—Dylan’s performance marked a turning point not only in his career but also for popular music: the boundaries between genres were blurring, and song lyrics were becoming more ambitious and literary.
