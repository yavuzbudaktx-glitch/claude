 1836: The Arc de Triomphe quietly opens

 On this day in 1836, the inauguration of the [Arc de Triomphe](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Arc-de-Triomphe), one of the largest [triumphal arches](https://www.britannica.com/topic/triumphal-arch) in the world, unfolded almost in secret. French King [Louis-Philippe](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Louis-Philippe), fearing assassination, cancelled the ceremony planned for one of Paris’s most ambitious monuments. Only 11 people attended. For a structure built to proclaim glory, its beginning was strikingly quiet.
