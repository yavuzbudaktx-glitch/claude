 1851: The Window Tax that backfired

 On this day in 1851,England abolished the despised Window Tax that had been in place since 1696. Because it felt too personal to ask how much someone made, the government developed a tax system that included counting [windows](https://www.britannica.com/technology/window). More windows generally meant a larger and more expensive house. But that was not always true, especially in urban areas. People resorted to boarding up windows, and new construction lacked ventilation. The government finally saw the light and ended the tax.
