 1945: First atomic bomb exploded near Alamogordo, New Mexico

 On this day in 1945 the [United States](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States) detonated the world’s first [atomic bomb](https://www.britannica.com/technology/atomic-bomb) during the [Trinity test](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Trinity-test) in the desert near [Alamogordo](https://www.britannica.com/place/Alamogordo), New Mexico. The successful culmination of the top secret [Manhattan Project](https://www.britannica.com/event/Manhattan-Project) marked the beginning of the atomic age and paved the way for the [bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki](https://www.britannica.com/event/atomic-bombings-of-Hiroshima-and-Nagasaki)—and the end of [World War II](https://www.britannica.com/event/World-War-II)—just weeks later.
