        <img loading="lazy" src="https://cdn.britannica.com/91/288691-050-24778A5D/today-in-history-federalist-papers.jpg?w=725&h=408&c=crop" alt="May 28, 1787: Publication of the essays that saved the Constitution" class="w-100"   />

                        <div class="card-body">
                            <div class="title font-18 font-weight-bold mb-10"><a href="https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/May-28-Publication-of-the-Federalist-Papers" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Publication of <em>The Federalist</em></a></div>
                            <div class="description font-serif">On this day, the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Federalist-papers" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Federalist papers</a>—a series of 77 essays defending the proposed new <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Constitution-of-the-United-States-of-America" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">U.S. Constitution</a> and discussing the nature of republican government, written in 1787–88 by <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/Alexander-Hamilton-United-States-statesman" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Alexander Hamilton</a>, <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/James-Madison" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">James Madison</a>, and <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/John-Jay" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">John Jay</a>—were published in book form as <em>The Federalist</em>. The essays explored a wide range of issues, including judicial review, foreign policy, taxation, militias, separation of powers, elections, treaties, and why the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Articles-of-Confederation" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Articles of Confederation</a> were not sufficient to hold the country together. The remaining eight essays appeared in New York newspapers between June 14 and August 16, 1788. <a class="otd-he-link font-weight-bold d-block mt-20 font-sans-serif" href="https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/May-28-Publication-of-the-Federalist-Papers">Read today's edition of Today in History &gt; &gt;</a></div>
