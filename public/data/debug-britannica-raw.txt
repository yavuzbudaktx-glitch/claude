 1040: Lady Godiva’s legendary ride

 According to legend, on this day in 1040, [Lady Godiva](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Lady-Godiva) rode horseback naked through the village of [Coventry](https://www.britannica.com/place/Coventry-England) to fulfill an agreement with her husband, [Leofric](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Leofric), the earl of [Mercia](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mercia) (a kingdom in what is now England), to reduce the heavy taxes imposed on their people. Also part of the legend? The first “Peeping Tom.” Read on to discover this date in Myth-Story.

 The [Scopes Trial](https://www.britannica.com/event/Scopes-Trial) began in Dayton, Tennessee, with [William Jennings Bryan](https://www.britannica.com/biography/William-Jennings-Bryan) arguing for the prosecution and [Clarence Darrow](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Clarence-Darrow) for the defense. _[[Read more about this and nine other “trials of the century.”](https://www.britannica.com/list/order-in-the-court-10-trials-of-the-century)]_

![Image 18: Wyoming](https://cdn.britannica.com/38/5238-050-C4B3E388/flag-citizen-Wyoming-response-contest-field-border-1917.jpg?w=260&h=196&c=crop)

 Encyclopædia Britannica, Inc.

1890

[Wyoming](https://www.britannica.com/place/Wyoming-state) is admitted as the United States’ 44th state. Wyoming’s constitution (1889) was the first in the world to grant full voting rights to women, as well as allow them to hold public office. Wyoming also became the first state to elect a woman governor when [Nellie Tayloe Ross](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Nellie-Tayloe-Ross) won the position in 1924. Because of these developments, Wyoming has been called the Equality State.
