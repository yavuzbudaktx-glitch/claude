 1778: The day the Liberty Bell came home

 On this day in 1778, a jubilant wagon procession escorted the [Liberty Bell](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Liberty-Bell) back to [Philadelphia](https://www.britannica.com/place/Philadelphia) after it had been hidden in [Allentown](https://www.britannica.com/place/Allentown), Pennsylvania, during the British occupation of the city. Fearing that British forces would melt the bell down for ammunition, Patriot officials had secretly removed it the previous year. Its triumphant return marked the end of an extraordinary wartime journey and preserved one of America’s most enduring symbols of liberty.
