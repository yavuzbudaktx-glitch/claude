 1992: The Canadian cod fishery collapse

 On this day in 1992, the [Canadian](https://www.britannica.com/place/Canada) government confirmed what thousands of people in[Newfoundland and Labrador](https://www.britannica.com/place/Newfoundland-and-Labrador)already knew:[Cod](https://www.britannica.com/animal/cod-fish-Gadus-species)fishing—and life as they knew it—was over. Officials announced a two-year moratorium on cod fishing to give the population stocks time to recover. The [collapse](https://www.britannica.com/event/cod-fishery-collapse-of-1992) had been decades in the making, thanks in large part to industrial fishing, poor management, and destructive and indiscriminate bottom trawling. The moratorium was repeatedly renewed until 2024, when a cautiously managed commercial cod fishery reopened.
