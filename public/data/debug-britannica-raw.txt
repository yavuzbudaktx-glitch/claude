## Featured Event

 1983

![Image 6: June 18, 1983: More than 20 years after the first Americans went to space, no American woman had been](https://cdn.britannica.com/93/289293-050-86DC19AC/today-in-history-Sally-Ride-becomes-first-US-woman-in-space.jpg?w=725&h=408&c=crop)

[Sally Ride became the first American woman in space](https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/June-18-The-First-American-Woman-in-Space)

When she was launched with four other astronauts aboard the space shuttle Challenger, Sally Ride became the first American woman to fly into outer space. Following Ride's death in 2012, obituaries revealed her long-term relationship with another woman, Tam O'Shaughnessy. Ride kept her sexual orientation private during her NASA career, but the reveal meant that, in 1983, she was also the first known [LGBTQ](https://www.britannica.com/topic/LGTBQ-community) astronaut to fly.
