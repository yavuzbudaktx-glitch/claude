 2007: A milestone moment for bald eagles

 This day in 2007 represented a rare conservation victory: [bald eagles](https://www.britannica.com/animal/bald-eagle) were removed from the U.S. [endangered species](https://www.britannica.com/science/endangered-species) list, capping one of the most dramatic wildlife comebacks in history.Just decades earlier, only 417 nesting pairs remained in the lower 48 states, their populations decimated by the pesticide [DDT](https://www.britannica.com/science/DDT). The harmful chemical, which made eggshells too thin to produce viable young, was banned in 1972, allowing bald eagles and countless other birds to recover.
