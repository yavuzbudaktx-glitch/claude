 1921: Baseball’s Black Sox trial begins

 On this day in 1921 the trial of baseball’s infamous [Black Sox](https://www.britannica.com/event/Black-Sox-Scandal) began. Eight [Chicago White Sox](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Chicago-White-Sox) players stood accused of accepting bribes to intentionally lose the 1919 [World Series](https://www.britannica.com/sports/World-Series). Although a jury acquitted them after key evidence disappeared, baseball’s commissioner permanently banned all eight from the sport, including legendary outfielder [Joe (“Shoeless Joe”) Jackson](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Shoeless-Joe-Jackson).

 © Alfredo Quant—AFP/Getty Images

[The end of the Football War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Football-War)

 The [Football War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Football-War), fought between [Honduras](https://www.britannica.com/place/Honduras) and [El Salvador](https://www.britannica.com/place/El-Salvador), came to an end on this day in 1969, four days after it began. It had significant human and economic costs, including the displacement of an estimated 300,000 Salvadorans, most of whom were expelled or fled from Honduras.
