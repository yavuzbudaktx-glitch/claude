 1992: Pablo Escobar escapes his own prison

 On this day in 1992, Colombian drug smuggler [Pablo Escobar](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Pablo-Escobar) escaped from a luxurious prison—that he had built. Known as La Catedral, the prison was part of a unique deal he had reached with the Colombian government. However, the arrangement unraveled when he killed two cartel members at La Catedral. Escobar disappeared as officials tried to move him to a military prison, and he eluded authorities until 1993, when his hideout was discovered and he died during the ensuing shootout.

Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel

Born this day in 1784, German astronomer [Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Friedrich-Wilhelm-Bessel) made the first measurement of the distance of a [star](https://www.britannica.com/science/star-astronomy) other than the [Sun](https://www.britannica.com/place/Sun), and his work allowed the first accurate determination of interstellar distances.
