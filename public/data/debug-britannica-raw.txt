      On this day in 1918 Tsar <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/Nicholas-II-tsar-of-Russia" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Nicholas II</a>, his wife, <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/Alexandra-empress-consort-of-Russia" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Alexandra</a>, their five children, and four loyal servants were executed by <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Bolshevik" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Bolshevik</a> forces in <a href="https://www.britannica.com/place/Yekaterinburg" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Yekaterinburg</a>.
