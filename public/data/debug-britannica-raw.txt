 1545: The sinking of the _Mary Rose_

 On this day in 1545 [Henry VIII](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Henry-VIII-king-of-England)’s flagship, the [Mary Rose](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Mary-Rose-English-warship), sank during the [Battle of the Solent](https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-the-Solent) after a gust of wind caused seawater to pour through its open gunports. More than 400 crew members died. Rediscovered in the [English Channel](https://www.britannica.com/place/English-Channel) centuries later, the remarkably preserved wreck became one of the world’s most important underwater archaeological discoveries.
