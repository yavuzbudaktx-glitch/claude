 1947: The beginning of Roswell UFO mania

 On this day in 1947, the _Roswell Daily Record_ erroneously reported that the previous day, soldiers at the [Roswell](https://www.britannica.com/place/Roswell-New-Mexico) Army Air Field in New Mexico had recovered the remains of a flying saucer that had crashed on a local ranch. Although that claim was quickly refuted by authorities, the Roswell incident eventually became the center of a [flying saucer](https://www.britannica.com/topic/unidentified-flying-object) mania that soon gripped the country.

Vasco da Gama’s first voyage to India

 On this day in 1497, [Vasco da Gama](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Vasco-da-Gama) sailed from [Lisbon](https://www.britannica.com/place/Lisbon) with a fleet of four vessels, and he ultimately opened a sea route from western [Europe](https://www.britannica.com/place/Europe) to [Asia](https://www.britannica.com/place/Asia) by way of the [Cape of Good Hope](https://www.britannica.com/place/Cape-of-Good-Hope), ushering in a new era in world history. _[[Take our quiz about explorers and exploration](https://www.britannica.com/quiz/explorers-and-explorations-part-one-quiz).]_
