 © The Picture Art Collection/Alamy; Photo Illustration Encyclopædia Britannica, Inc.

[The outbreak of the dancing plague](https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/June-24-1374-Dancing-Plague-of-Aachen)

 On this day in 1374, men, women, and children gathered in the streets of [Aachen](https://www.britannica.com/place/Aachen), [Germany](https://www.britannica.com/place/Germany), and danced as if in a trance. They continued for hours—even days—moving until they collapsed from exhaustion. This was earliest widely documented (and worst) major outbreak of choreomania, the dancing plague, a form of mass psychogenic illness. Other outbreaks occurred elsewhere in Europe in later decades and centuries, but no other major cases of choreomania have been reported since the mid-1600s._[[Read today’s edition of Today in History >>](https://www.britannica.com/today-in-history/June-24-1374-Dancing-Plague-of-Aachen)]_

## More on June 24

![Image 8: supporters and opponents of abortion bans outside the U.S. Supreme Court](https://cdn.britannica.com/72/204572-050-4708CA63/Abortion-demonstration-United-States-Supreme-Court-building-Washington-DC-1989.jpg?w=260&h=196&c=crop)

2022

 In [_Dobbs_ v. _Jackson Women’s Health Organization_](https://www.britannica.com/event/Dobbs-v-Jackson-Womens-Health-Organization), the [U.S. Supreme Court](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Supreme-Court-of-the-United-States) overturned [_Roe_ v. _Wade_](https://www.britannica.com/event/Roe-v-Wade) (1973), ending the constitutional right to an [abortion](https://www.britannica.com/science/abortion-pregnancy). _[[Why are there nine justices on the U.S. Supreme Court?](https://www.britannica.com/story/why-are-there-nine-justices-on-the-us-supreme-court)]_

2010

1948

 The [Berlin blockade](https://www.britannica.com/event/Berlin-blockade) intensified when the [Soviet Union](https://www.britannica.com/place/Soviet-Union) announced that the Western [Allied powers](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Allied-Powers-international-alliance) no longer had any rights in [Berlin](https://www.britannica.com/place/Berlin). _[[Watch an overview of the Soviet Union’s blockade of West Berlin](https://www.britannica.com/event/Berlin-blockade/images-videos#/media/1/62154/194399).]_

1947

 American businessman Kenneth Arnold saw a number of objects “flying like saucers” while piloting a small plane over Mount Rainier in Washington; it was considered the first modern sighting of [UFOs](https://www.britannica.com/topic/unidentified-flying-object) and gave rise to the term _flying saucer_.

1932

 The [Promoters Revolution](https://www.britannica.com/event/Promoters-Revolution), a bloodless coup, overthrew [Prajadhipok](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Prajadhipok), the king of [Thailand](https://www.britannica.com/place/Thailand), ending the absolute monarchy in that country and initiating the so-called Constitutional Era. _[[Sort fact from fiction in our quiz about Thailand](https://www.britannica.com/quiz/exploring-thailand-fact-or-fiction).]_
