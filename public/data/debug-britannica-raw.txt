 1957: Althea Gibson wins Wimbledon

 On this day in 1957, American tennis player [Althea Gibson](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Althea-Gibson) became the first Black player to win the women’s singles title at Wimbledon, where in 1951, she was the first Black player to compete at the tournament. Between 1956 and 1958, Gibson appeared in 19 major finals—including singles, doubles, and mixed doubles—and won 11 titles. She won the singles title again at [Wimbledon](https://www.britannica.com/sports/Wimbledon-Championships) in 1958.

Anne Frank forced into hiding

[Anne Frank](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Anne-Frank) and her family went into hiding in Amsterdam on this day in 1942 and lived in a secret annex—an experience documented in her [diary](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Diary-of-a-Young-Girl), which became a classic of war literature—until their capture on August 4, 1944. _[[Take a tour of the Anne Frank House in Amsterdam](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Anne-Frank/images-videos#/media/1/217168/192171).]_
