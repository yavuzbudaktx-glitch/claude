 1835: A war fought over Toledo

 The only bloodshed during the [Toledo War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Toledo-War), fought between [Ohio](https://www.britannica.com/place/Ohio-state) and [Michigan](https://www.britannica.com/place/Michigan) over a disputed border, occurred on this day in a [Toledo](https://www.britannica.com/place/Toledo-Ohio) tavern when Ohio partisan Two Stickney stabbed Michigan Sheriff Joseph Wood with a penknife. (Wood survived.) The “war” ended two years later with Toledo awarded to Ohio and the resource-rich Upper Peninsula to Michigan.

[Mariner 4 photographed Mars](https://www.britannica.com/technology/Mariner)

 On this day in 1965 [Mariner](https://www.britannica.com/technology/Mariner) 4, an uncrewed space probe launched by [NASA](https://www.britannica.com/topic/NASA) in 1964, flew by [Mars](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mars-planet) and returned close-up pictures of its surface. The pictures proved that the [planet’s so-called canals](https://www.britannica.com/place/canals-of-Mars) were illusions. _[[Sort fact from fiction in our quiz about Mars](https://www.britannica.com/quiz/mars-fact-or-fiction).]_
