 1948: Berlin airlift takes flight

 On this day, the United States and Britain began supplying [West Berlin](https://www.britannica.com/place/West-Berlin) with food and other vital necessities by air after the Soviet Union’s [Berlin blockade](https://www.britannica.com/event/Berlin-blockade) cut off all land and water access to the city. What started as a risky response to a [Cold War](https://www.britannica.com/event/Cold-War) crisis became one of the largest aid missions in history, as Allied aircraft delivered food, fuel, medicine, and other essentials to the city’s 2.5 million residents for 11 months.
