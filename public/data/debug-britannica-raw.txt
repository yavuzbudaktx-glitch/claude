 1948: The Battle of London Airport

 On this day in 1948, an audacious robbery attempt was foiled at the newly opened London Airport (better known as [Heathrow](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Heathrow-Airport)). With help from an inside man, robbers had planned to steal at least a thousand pounds’ worth of gold bullion arriving from South America. The inside man changed his mind, however, and called in the [Scotland Yard](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Scotland-Yard) Flying Squad. Read on to discover what happened next.
