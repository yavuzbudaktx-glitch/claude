                            <div class="description font-serif">On this day in 1895, Irish poet and dramatist <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/Oscar-Wilde" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Oscar Wilde</a> was convicted of “committing acts of gross indecency with certain male persons.” The charge stemmed from his relationships with young men, most notably <a href="https://www.britannica.com/biography/Lord-Alfred-Douglas" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Lord Alfred (Bosie) Douglas</a>.
