    <div class="font-newsreader tw:text-lg tw:mb-8 tw:max-md:mb-4 tw:leading-[29px]">
      On this day in 1776, the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Declaration-of-Independence" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Declaration of Independence</a>&nbsp;was approved by the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/Continental-Congress" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">Continental Congress</a>. The document—which announced the separation of the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/topic/American-colonies" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">13 North American British&nbsp;colonies</a>&nbsp;from Great Britain—was met with scorn by British officials. Fighting dragged on for years, and when the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/event/American-Revolution" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">American Revolution</a> finally ended in 1783, the colonies were victorious and went on to form the <a href="https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States" class="md-crosslink " data-show-preview="true">United States of America</a>. July 4 subsequently became a national holiday.</div>
